Flagship project

Deephedging

A neural network learns to hedge a short option by minimising the risk of its hedged P&L on simulated paths. No pricing formula, no Greeks, no continuous trading. Written from scratch in NumPy, backpropagation through time included, and benchmarked against Black-Scholes, Leland and Whalley-Wilmott.
